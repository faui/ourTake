# Audit 02 — Brand Kit & Logo Exploration

Audit date: 2026-09-10 · Auditor: Claude (handoff audit) · Scope: `docs/BRAND-KIT.md`, `docs/brand-exploration/*`, brand build scripts, `public/*`, and brand usage in the shipped app. Read-only; no originals modified.

## 1. Current state of the brand kit (`docs/BRAND-KIT.md`)

**Decided (explicitly "Agreed"):**
- **Name:** `ourTake`, exact capitalization, founder-selected; supersedes OurFrame/OurTake/takeO (BRAND-KIT.md:3). Trademark/domain clearance explicitly NOT done (:3, :152).
- **Essence & narrative:** "shared creative play"; capture a take → bring together our takes → discover another take (:14–18).
- **Seven ranked tenets** (togetherness #1 → contemporary expression #7) with design consequences and hard accessibility gates (small-size, contrast, mono, reduced motion) declared non-negotiable (:26–38).
- **Composition naming:** numbered takes with descriptive suffixes (`Take 1 — The whole story`), numbering semantics specified (:42–46).
- **Evaluation method:** every candidate must work across four surfaces (app icon, invitation, composition attribution, motion) (:56).

**Exploratory (explicitly not decided):**
- Every mark, palette, letterform, motion treatment, slogan (:8). Family B "Pocket play" is named the leading hypothesis, not a winner (:8, :63). Tagline is a candidate list, no master selected (:22).
- The O/T monogram study is flagged as "permission to investigate, not selection" (:70).
- Wordmark is provisional system type everywhere (:64, and stated on the study PNGs).

**Retained pilot system (pre-decision operational identity):** OurFrame frame-and-dot mark, lime/ink dark palette (9 tokens with hex + role table, :100–113), Arial/system type stack with scale (:116–120), layout/interaction and copy principles (:123–148), four edition recipes (:135–144). The kit is unusually disciplined about decided-vs-exploratory and anti-overclaiming (no fake AI/sync claims, :24, :68).

## 2. Logo exploration assessment

**Round 01 families** (generated by `scripts/build-brand-concepts.mjs`, board `docs/brand-exploration/index.html`, contact sheet `overview.png`):

| Candidate | Description | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|---|---|
| **a-mark** (Shared aperture) | Two interleaved rounded brackets forming a shared center; rust `#B53F2C` + teal `#305A68`, stroke-based | Instant camera/frame association; simplest geometry; reads clearly at 16 px; clean motion story (brackets arrive, settle) | Generic — risks reading as crop/focus utility software (kit admits this, :62); low distinctiveness ceiling |
| **b-mark** (Pocket play) | Three bent "elbow" pieces rotated 120° around a shared pocket; indigo `#4437B6` + rust | Strongest link to the play/recombination product behavior; has a dedicated **micro variant** (`b-mark-micro.svg`) with thickened simplified paths — best small-size engineering in the set | Puzzle/recycling-arrows association risk; detail loss without the micro variant; most abstract of the three |
| **c-mark** (Common thread, study 02) | Two broad strands crossing over/under with real gaps, forming a woven window; deep blue `#214F68` + clay `#BB4C34` | Strongest togetherness+craft metaphor; crossings are genuine transparent geometry (explicit stroke breaks, no masks/knockouts — build-brand-concepts.mjs:20–22); works one-color; distinctive silhouette | Densest mark — at 16 px the gaps nearly close (visible in `common-thread-study.png` small-size check); textile/knot/craft-service association risk |
| **c-previous mark** | Single cursive open-loop stroke (retained sketch) | Warm, human, simple | Reads as generic "connection squiggle"/infinity-adjacent; correctly retired to comparison-only status |
| **ot-counter** (O/T study 01) | Solid circle with T-shaped negative aperture (single evenodd path) | Best negative-space idea; bulletproof at any size; single-path = ideal favicon material | Reads as a "T badge" first; weave character mostly lost (kit and study are honest about this) |
| **ot-threaded** (O/T study 02) | O strand and T strand sharing one over/under crossing | Best continuity with the weave thesis; letters emerge as a rewarding second read | Open O can read as C; two-stroke construction thinner at 16 px |
| **ot-loom** (O/T study 03, "Open initials") | O and T both cut from one rounded tile (evenodd cutouts) | Maximum letter legibility; genuine cutouts, works reversed | Tiny counters + floating O island are fragile at small sizes — self-described as "a lettering control, not the strongest weave direction" |
| **Previous mark (production)** | `public/ourframe-mark.svg` — charcoal rounded square, lime capture corners, center dot | Legible, functional, matches shipped dark UI | Belongs to the superseded OurFrame identity; generic "screenshot/scan corners" gesture |

**Technical quality of the SVGs — high for exploration stage:**
- Every candidate ships color + mono pairs; monos use `currentColor` (hardcoded fallback `style="color:#22252B"`), so they recolor cleanly.
- Real vector transparency throughout (no white knockouts), `pathLength="100"` on C enables the draw-on motion; `fill-rule="evenodd"` on the O/T marks is correct.
- The board (`index.html`) is genuinely well-built: family tabs with `aria-pressed`, live-region status, monochrome toggle, portrait toggle, replayable 1.4–1.5 s motion, `prefers-reduced-motion` kill-switch, actual 16/24/48 px specimens, focus-visible outlines. `ot-weave.html` adds letter-reading reveal + dark-reverse toggles.
- Everything regenerates deterministically from two scripts (marks, vocabulary sheets, board, PNG contact sheets via sharp) — single source of truth, no drift between board and downloadable assets.
- Minor nits: standalone `*-mark.svg` files lack `role="img"`/`<title>` (the ot-* files have aria-labels); stray empty `class=""` attributes; wordmarks are system-font (Arial/Segoe UI) styled text, acknowledged as provisional.

## 3. Gap analysis toward "finalize logo and targeted visual iconography"

Missing, roughly in dependency order:
1. **Final mark selection** — no winner among A/B/C(+O/T); the kit requires audience validation (recall, first-impression meaning, category confusion, cross-market interpretation) that hasn't happened (:56–58).
2. **Trademark/domain/distinctiveness clearance** — explicitly outstanding (:3, :58, :152).
3. **Custom wordmark** — all lockups use system type; a drawn/licensed wordmark and mark-wordmark lockup spec (clear space, minimum sizes) don't exist for the new identity (clear-space rules exist only for the legacy OurFrame mark, :98).
4. **Production icon set** — no favicon.ico/PNG ladder (16/32/180/192/512), no maskable/adaptive Android icon, no iOS squircle-safe padding tests, no real-device icon review (:58). Manifest has a single SVG "any" icon.
5. **Social/OG assets** — no OG image, no `metadata.openGraph` in `app/layout.tsx`, no avatar/profile crops.
6. **Typography decision + licensing** — kit only says "choose a licensed modern grotesk, self-host WOFF2" later (:120); no candidate shortlist.
7. **Final color system** — three competing exploratory palettes plus the operational lime/ink set; no accessibility contrast documentation anywhere (lime `#D8FC71` on `#0d1011` passes trivially, but candidate palettes like `#B53F2C` on `#FFF3E5` are near the 4.5:1 line and unmeasured).
8. **Icon system** — the "candidate visual vocabulary" sheets exist per family, but the shipped app uses stock lucide icons; no plan mapping family-native forms to UI icon language.
9. **Motion spec** — a good proposal exists (1.2–1.8 s, once-triggered, reduced-motion still, :68) but no production easing/duration tokens or Lottie/CSS deliverables.
10. **Dark/light mode rules** — board shows light alternatives and dark-reverse toggles, but no reversal rules; the shipped app is dark-only (`color-scheme:dark`, globals.css:8).
11. **Usage/misuse rules** for the future identity (only legacy-mark rules exist, :98) and migration plan for the OurFrame runtime debt (:3 says "migration is separate work" — unscheduled).

## 5. Scored verdict

- **Brand-kit completeness: 6/10** — strategy, naming, principles, voice, and decision discipline are unusually rigorous, but the identity layer (final mark, wordmark, palette, type licensing, OG/icon/social assets, contrast documentation, clearance) is entirely unresolved by design.
- **Logo-exploration quality: 8/10** — three genuinely distinct theses plus a negative-space sub-study, each with color/mono/micro variants, real 16/24/48 px specimens, four-surface contexts, motion, honest critiques, and fully regenerable tooling; docked for system-font wordmarks, C's near-closing gaps at 16 px, zero audience validation, and no production-format exports.
